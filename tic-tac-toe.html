Tic-Tac-Toe: Guide & Rules

Tic-Tac-Toe on a 3 x 3 grid, either against the computer or against a friend on the same device. In the computer mode you play X and move first, and the opponent can be set to Easy, Medium or Invincible. Get three in a row, across, down or diagonally, before your opponent does.

How to play Tic-Tac-Toe

X always moves first. In the Vs Computer mode you are X and the computer plays O after a short pause. In the 2 Players mode, X and O take turns on the same screen, so you can pass the device back and forth with a friend.

The three difficulty levels change how the computer chooses its moves. Invincible uses the minimax method, which looks ahead through every possible continuation, so the best you can do against it is a draw. Medium plays the best move about 65% of the time and a random legal move otherwise. Easy always plays a random legal move.

When a round ends the board stays as it is until you press the new round button. The tally of X wins, ties and O wins is kept while the page stays open. Changing mode or difficulty gives you a fresh board but keeps the tally. Reloading the page or leaving the game resets it.

How a round ends

A round ends when one player has three marks in a row (any of the 3 rows, 3 columns or 2 diagonals), or when all nine cells are filled with no winning line, which is a draw.

The status line announces the result: "YOU WON!" or "COMPUTER WINS!" in the computer mode, "PLAYER X WINS!" or "PLAYER O WINS!" in the 2 Players mode, or "IT'S A DRAW!".

Special features

  • Two modes. Play the computer or a friend on the same device.
  • Three difficulty levels in the computer mode.
  • Highlighted winning line when a round is won.
  • The mode is local to your device. There is no online multiplayer.

Tips for beginners

  • You move first as X, so you can claim the centre or a corner on your first turn.
  • On every turn, check whether your opponent has two in a line with an empty third cell, and block it.
  • Against Invincible, aim not to lose. A draw is the best possible result.
  • Use Easy and Medium to practise spotting two-way threats, where one move creates two possible lines at once.
  • Use the 2 Players mode to keep a running tally against a friend over several rounds.

Good to know

  • In the computer mode on Medium and Invincible, three cells can sometimes glow as if a line had been completed while the round is still going. It is only a display effect. The round is over only when the status line announces a result.

Tic-Tac-Toe FAQ

Can I beat the Invincible computer?

No. It checks every possible continuation, so the best result you can get against it is a draw.

Can I play as O or move second?

Not in the computer mode. You are always X and you always move first.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. Pick 2 PLAYERS, and take turns on the same device. There is no online multiplayer.

Is my score saved?

No. The win tally lasts only while the page stays open, and there is no best score in this game.

What happens when I change difficulty?

The board is cleared and a new round starts, but the tally stays.

How does Medium play?

It chooses the best move about 65% of the time and a random move the rest of the time.
