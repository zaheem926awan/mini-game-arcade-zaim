1. Who we are

Neo Arcade Vault (“we”, “us”) is a free browser-game website available at mini-game-arcade-zaim.vercel.app. The site is operated by Neo Arcade Vault, an independent project based in Pakistan.

You can contact us about this policy using the details in section 12.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account to play, and the games do not ask for your name or email address.
  • Game progress (high scores, favorites, settings) is saved in your browser on your own device.
  • We use Google Analytics 4 to understand website traffic, visitor behavior, device usage and popular pages. Google may collect usage data through cookies and similar technologies for this purpose (see section 13).
  • At the date of this policy, the site’s own game code does not set cookies and the site does not display advertising.
  • If you use the contact form, the details you type are processed by a third-party form service, Formspree, and sent to us.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is delivered by our hosting provider, Vercel; both can see technical information such as your IP address (see section 5).
  • We have applied to Google AdSense and are waiting for Google’s review and approval. Advertising is not running yet; before it is enabled we will update this policy and set up consent tools where they are required (see section 6).

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the site uses your browser’s local storage. This data stays in your browser; our code does not send it to us or to any other server. The following items are saved:

Stored item (key)What it is forKept
arcade_favoritesThe list of games you marked as favorites.Until you remove it
arcade_recently_playedThe list of games you opened recently.Until you remove it

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser settings (often found under “Privacy” or “Site settings”). Doing so resets your scores, favorites and settings. Private-browsing modes usually discard this data when the window is closed.

If advertising or consent tools are added in the future, they may store their own data in your browser separately from the items above; we will describe this in section 6 before they are enabled.

4. Information you send us

If you use the contact form, we receive the information you enter: your name, email address, chosen topic and message, and your confirmation that you have read this policy. We use it to read and reply to your message, to handle any privacy or legal matter connected with it, and to keep a record of the conversation for the period in section 7.

The form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service, which receives each submission on our behalf and forwards it to us. Formspree may also receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when you submit the form. Formspree has its own privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

The form includes a hidden spam-protection field that real visitors never see or fill in. If you email us directly at zaim926awan@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

5. Third-party services

Hosting (Vercel)

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver the site, keep it secure and keep server logs. See Vercel’s privacy policy: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Contact form (Formspree)

Messages sent through the contact form are processed by Formspree, as described in section 4.

Google Fonts

Our pages load the “Outfit” and “Luckiest Guy” fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand website traffic, visitor behavior, device usage and popular pages. Google may collect usage data through cookies and similar technologies. See section 13 for details and how to opt out.

External links

The site may link to other websites. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Advertising and cookies (planned)

Current status: at the date of this policy, the site does not display advertising, and no consent-management tool is installed. Our own game code does not set cookies, but Google Analytics may set cookies or use similar technologies (see below and section 13). Google AdSense code is installed on the website, but no advertisements are currently displayed because the website is awaiting AdSense approval.

We have applied to Google AdSense and are currently waiting for Google’s website review and approval. This has not been enabled and approval is not guaranteed. Before any advertising goes live, we will update this policy and put the consent setup described below in place.

Google Analytics cookies. Google Analytics 4 uses cookies and similar technologies, such as first-party cookies with names starting “_ga”, to recognize a browser across visits and to measure how the site is used. These are set by Google Analytics, not by our own game code, and they are not used by us to show advertising. You can block or delete them in your browser settings or use the opt-out described in section 13.

If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a user’s previous visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You will be able to opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires consent before advertising cookies are used (for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), we intend to use Google’s Privacy & messaging tools to request it. No consent-management tool is installed on the site at the date of this policy. We will describe how you can review or change your choices here once such a tool is in place.

7. How long we keep information

Data stored in your browser stays there until you delete it. Contact messages are kept for 12 months and then deleted, except where we need to keep them longer for legal reasons or where it is reasonably necessary, for example to resolve a dispute, deal with a security matter or follow up on a privacy request. Formspree and our email provider may hold copies of messages as described in their own policies.

8. Your rights and choices

You can clear your locally stored game data at any time (see section 3) and you can choose not to use the contact form.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information we hold about you, such as the right to ask for access, correction or deletion, to object to or restrict certain use, or to complain to your local data-protection authority. Which rights apply depends on the law where you live. To make a request, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”, or email zaim926awan@gmail.com.

9. Children

Neo Arcade Vault is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to use the site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and children under 13 should not use the contact form.

In some countries the minimum age for agreeing to the handling of personal data is higher than 13. If that applies to you, please ask a parent or guardian before using the contact form. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security and international transfers

The site is delivered over HTTPS by our hosting provider. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. The services described above (Vercel, Formspree and Google) may process data in countries other than your own, including outside Pakistan.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or services. We will update it before we enable advertising. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Please check this page occasionally.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy, use our contact form or email zaim926awan@gmail.com.

13. Google Analytics

Neo Arcade Vault uses Google Analytics 4 (“Google Analytics”), a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how the site is used. It helps us see website traffic, visitor behavior, the devices people use and which pages and games are most popular, so that we can improve the site.

To do this, Google may collect usage data through cookies and similar technologies. This can include the pages you view, how long you stay, how you arrived at the site, your approximate location, and technical details such as your browser, device type and screen size. Google may also process your IP address for this purpose. We use this information in aggregated form and do not use Google Analytics to identify you personally.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can read more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.
