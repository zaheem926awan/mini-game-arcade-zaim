Tips for the best experience

  • Use a modern, up-to-date browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  • On phones, turn your device sideways for wider games such as Space Shooter and Endless Runner.
  • Not sure how a game works? Open its Guide & Rules page for controls, scoring and tips.
  • Use the fullscreen button in the game view for a larger play area.
