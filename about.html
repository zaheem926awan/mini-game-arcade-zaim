About Neo Arcade Vault

Neo Arcade Vault is a free collection of ten browser-based mini-games. Open the site, pick a game and start playing in seconds on your phone, tablet or computer — no downloads and no account required.

Our mission

Short, satisfying games should be easy to reach. We built Neo Arcade Vault to bring a handful of classic arcade ideas — slicing, snaking, breaking bricks, merging tiles, stacking blocks and more — together in one fast, good-looking place that works on whatever device you happen to have in your hand.

Every game is designed for quick sessions: you can play for two minutes while waiting for a bus, or keep chasing a high score for an evening. Where it makes sense, games include touch controls, keyboard controls or both, so they feel natural on any screen.

How the site is built

Neo Arcade Vault is a static website written with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The games are drawn with the HTML5 Canvas or with standard page elements, and sound comes from the Web Audio API. There is no framework, no build step and nothing to install, which keeps pages light and fast.

The project includes automated tests for the game logic, such as scoring, collisions, the Tic-Tac-Toe AI and the puzzle rules, to help catch mistakes when games are updated.

Some of the site’s code and content was created with the help of AI tools. The two typefaces used on the site, Outfit and Luckiest Guy, are third-party fonts loaded from Google Fonts under their own open licences — see our Terms & Conditions for details.
