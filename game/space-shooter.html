About Space Shooter

Space Shooter puts you in a starfighter with three shield points. Drones, heavy cruisers and asteroids come down the screen in busier and busier waves, and only the cruisers shoot back.

Rare power-ups add a triple shot, a shield point or a bomb that clears the screen the moment you touch it.

Space Shooter is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score is saved on your own device when a run ends. It is one of 3 action games in the vault, together with Fruit Slice and Endless Runner. See the Action games or the full Neo Arcade Vault games collection.

Quick start for Space Shooter

Your ship starts at the bottom centre with twin cannons. In the first wave enemies arrive about every 1.4 seconds, which leaves time to line up each orange cruiser: three hits, 50 points, and the only enemy that shoots back. Waves speed up the arrivals as your score rises.

Quick tips for Space Shooter

  • Shoot cruisers first. They are the only enemies that fire back, and they take three hits.
  • Do not ram enemies. A collision costs a shield point and scores nothing.
  • Letting an enemy slip off the bottom is free, so never risk your shield for a drone you cannot reach.

Space Shooter questions answered

Which enemy should I shoot first?

The heavy cruiser. It is the only enemy that fires back, and it takes three hits to destroy, so it is worth dealing with early.

What do the enemy colours mean?

Red drones take one hit and score 20 points, orange heavy cruisers take three hits and score 50, and grey asteroids take two hits and score 30.

How long does the triple shot last?

A triple shot lasts 9 seconds. About 16% of destroyed enemies drop a power-up, and you collect it by flying into it.

What happens when I get hit?

A cruiser bolt or a collision costs 1 shield point and gives you 1.2 seconds of invulnerability. That window is best used to reposition rather than to take more risk.
