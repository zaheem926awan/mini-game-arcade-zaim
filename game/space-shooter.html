About Space Shooter

Space Shooter puts you in a starfighter with three shield points. Drones, heavy cruisers and asteroids come down the screen in busier and busier waves, and only the cruisers shoot back.

Rare power-ups add a triple shot, a shield point or a bomb that clears the screen the moment you touch it.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Quick tips for Space Shooter

  • Shoot cruisers first. They are the only enemies that fire back, and they take three hits.
  • Do not ram enemies. A collision costs a shield point and scores nothing.
  • Letting an enemy slip off the bottom is free, so never risk your shield for a drone you cannot reach.
