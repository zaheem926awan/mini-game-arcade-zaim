How to play Space Shooter online

Arrow Keys / WASD / Drag to fly, Space to fire, Shift for your ability.

On a computer, fly with the arrow keys or WASD, hold Space to fire and press Shift or E for your special ability. The mouse also flies the ship, and holding the left button fires. On a phone, drag to fly (the ship fires automatically) and use the on-screen Ability button.

About Space Shooter

Space Shooter is a vertical shooter campaign. Fly a starfighter through six sectors, from the Dust Belt to the Void Core, shooting drones, weavers, kamikazes, gunships, snipers, asteroids and carriers. Every sector ends with a boss fight that has its own attack patterns.

Coins from enemies and missions unlock five ships and upgrade damage, fire rate, shield, speed and your special ability. Power-ups add rapid fire, spread shots, an overshield, repairs and ability charges.

Space Shooter is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your coins, ships, upgrades, missions and medals are saved on your own device. It is one of 10 free games in the Neo Arcade Vault games collection.

Quick start for Space Shooter

Press Launch and your Vanguard starts at the bottom centre with twin cannons and a shield of 3 points. The first mission asks you to destroy 25 enemies. Pick up the gold coins enemies drop, then spend them in the hangar on new ships and upgrades before the next sector.

Quick tips for Space Shooter

  • Shoot snipers and gunships first. They are the enemies that fire back, and snipers show a warning line before they shoot.
  • Do not ram enemies. A collision costs a shield point, so slip past anything you cannot destroy in time.
  • Spend your first coins on a Shield Strength upgrade: more shield points forgive early mistakes while you learn each boss pattern.
