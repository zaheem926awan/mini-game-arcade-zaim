About Neon Snake

Neon Snake moves one cell per step on a 22 x 22 board with solid walls. Every apple makes the snake longer and the game faster, and one crash ends the run.

After every fifth apple a golden star appears for six seconds, worth as much as five apples. There is no wrap-around and no difficulty menu, just you, the grid and a growing tail.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Quick tips for Neon Snake

  • The first apples are the slowest part of a run. Settle into a steady rhythm before the speed rises.
  • Tap a direction once and wait for the snake to move. Only one pending turn is stored per step.
  • As the snake grows, keep an open lane to turn into. Long snakes end runs far more often than walls do.
