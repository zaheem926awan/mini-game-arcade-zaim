About Whack-a-Mole

Whack-a-Mole is a 30-second reflex round on a 3 x 3 board. Moles and golden stars pop out of the holes for a moment and score when you hit them, and a combo multiplies the points.

Bombs reset your combo, so a quick eye matters as much as a quick hand. A mouse or a finger is all you need.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Quick tips for Whack-a-Mole

  • Missed moles are free, so skip a hole if hitting it would mean clicking a bomb.
  • Hit golden stars first. They disappear soonest and are worth the most.
  • Once your combo reaches 4, every hit is worth double, so protect that streak.
