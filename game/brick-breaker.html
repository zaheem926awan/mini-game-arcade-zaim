About Brick Breaker

Brick Breaker gives you three lives and three stages of bricks. Move the paddle with the mouse, the keyboard or a finger and keep the ball in play while it chips away at the rows above.

Bricks that need several hits show a number. Falling capsules can add extra balls, widen the paddle, give bonus points or slow the ball down.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Brick Breaker controls

Left / Right arrows or A / DMove the paddle.

TouchDrag a finger across the board, or hold the on-screen move buttons.

Quick tips for Brick Breaker

  • Hit the ball with the paddle edge to send it sideways and reach bricks in the corners.
  • With multi-ball you only lose a life when the last ball falls, so it is the safest capsule to chase.
  • Check the numbers on bricks. A 3-hit gold brick takes three hits, so do not leave it behind.
