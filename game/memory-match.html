About Memory Match

Memory Match lays out 16 face-down cards: eight pairs of arcade symbols. Flip two at a time, remember where each symbol is hiding and clear the board.

There is no time limit. Your result is rated from one to three stars by the number of moves you needed, and your fewest moves is saved on this device.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Quick tips for Memory Match

  • Early flips are for gathering information. Every card you see makes a later match easier.
  • Use rows and columns to remember positions, for example "top row, second card".
  • Stars depend on moves and not on time, so there is no reason to rush.
