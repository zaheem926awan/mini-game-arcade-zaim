About Tic-Tac-Toe

Tic-Tac-Toe here has two modes: a computer opponent set to Easy, Medium or Invincible, or two players sharing one device. In the computer mode you play X and move first.

A win tally is kept while the page stays open, and a completed line is highlighted so you can see exactly how the round ended.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Quick tips for Tic-Tac-Toe

  • You move first as X, so you can claim the centre or a corner on your first turn.
  • Every turn, check whether your opponent has two in a line with an empty third cell, and block it.
  • Against Invincible, aim not to lose. A draw is the best possible result.
