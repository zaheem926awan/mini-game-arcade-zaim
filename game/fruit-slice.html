About Fruit Slice

Fruit Slice runs on a 60-second clock. Fruit is thrown up from the bottom of the screen, every slice scores, and slicing several pieces in one stroke earns combo bonuses.

Bombs are the only way to lose one of your three lives, so half the skill is knowing when not to swipe. The start screen lets you switch bombs off for a calmer round and choose a blade colour.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Quick tips for Fruit Slice

  • Combos count fruits per swipe, so one long stroke through a cluster beats several short ones.
  • A missed fruit costs nothing, while a bomb costs points, time and a life. Do not lunge for everything.
  • Golden apples are rare and give +5 points and 2 extra seconds, so they deserve a deliberate swipe.
