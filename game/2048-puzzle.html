About 2048 Puzzle

2048 Puzzle is played on a 4 x 4 board. Each move slides every tile one way and merges equal neighbours into a tile of double the value, and a new tile appears after every move that works.

Reach 2048 to win, or keep going for a higher score. If a move goes wrong, the one-step undo can take it back.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Quick tips for 2048 Puzzle

  • Undo right after a move you regret. It only goes back one move and cannot be used twice in a row.
  • Before each move, look for rows and columns with pairs that will merge, because each merge scores the new tile value.
  • A new tile appears after every move that works, so avoid filling the board with tiles that do not match.
