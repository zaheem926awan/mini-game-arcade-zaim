About Endless Runner

In Endless Runner your character runs forward on its own and you decide when to jump, double jump or slide. Spikes need a jump, laser drones need a slide, and energy crystals add to your score.

You have a single life, so one collision ends the run. The challenge is simply how far you can get.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Endless Runner controls

InputWhat it does
Space, Up arrow or WJump. Press again in the air for a double jump.
Down arrow or S (hold)Slide. Release to stand up again.
P or EscPause and resume.
TouchTap the game area to jump, or use the JUMP and SLIDE buttons.

Quick tips for Endless Runner

  • Start your slide just before a drone reaches you and hold it until it has passed.
  • Use the double jump on a tight spike so you do not land on the next obstacle.
  • Crystals are worth a small detour, but never a collision.
