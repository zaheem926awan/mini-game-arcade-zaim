About Endless Runner

In Endless Runner your character runs forward on its own and you decide when to jump, double jump or slide. Spikes need a jump, laser drones need a slide, and energy crystals add to your score.

You have a single life, so one collision ends the run. The challenge is simply how far you can get.

Endless Runner is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score is saved on your own device during the run. It is one of 3 action games in the vault, together with Fruit Slice and Space Shooter. See the Action games or the full Neo Arcade Vault games collection.

Quick start for Endless Runner

The runner starts at a steady pace and gets faster as your distance grows, up to a top speed. Your score is distance plus 25 points for every energy crystal, so a trail of three is worth 75. The hitbox is slightly forgiving, so very close misses do not always count as hits.

Quick tips for Endless Runner

  • Start your slide just before a drone reaches you and hold it until it has passed.
  • Use the double jump on a tight spike so you do not land on the next obstacle.
  • Crystals are worth a small detour, but never a collision.

Endless Runner questions answered

How fast does the runner get?

Speed rises with your distance until it reaches a top speed after about 4,400 m, and the gaps between obstacles get shorter at higher speeds.

Are close calls forgiven?

Slightly. The collision box is a little forgiving, so very close misses do not always count as hits.

Where do the energy crystals appear?

About 70% of the time a trail of three crystals follows an obstacle. After a drone they sit near the ground, and after a spike they hang at jump height. Each crystal is worth 25 points.

What is the easiest way to play on a phone?

Tap the game area to jump, which is quicker than reaching for the JUMP button, and hold the SLIDE button to pass under drones.
