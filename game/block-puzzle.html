About Block Puzzle

Block Puzzle drops seven shapes onto a 10 x 20 board, one at a time. Move and rotate each piece so that it completes a full row, which then clears.

A ghost outline shows where the piece will land, one piece can be held for later and a hard drop locks it instantly. The pace quickens every ten lines, and the run ends when a new piece no longer fits.

It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or account. Your best score stays on your own device.

Block Puzzle controls

InputWhat it does
Left / Right arrows or A / DMove the piece one cell sideways.
Up arrow or WRotate clockwise.
SpaceHard drop: send the piece to its landing spot.
C or ShiftHold or swap the held piece.
P or EscPause and resume.

Quick tips for Block Puzzle

  • Use the ghost outline to check the landing spot before you drop.
  • You can hold only once per turn, so keep the hold for a piece you really cannot place.
  • Keep the stack low and flat. The game is over as soon as a new piece cannot fit at the top.
