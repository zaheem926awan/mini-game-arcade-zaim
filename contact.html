Before you write

How do I report a bug?

Choose “Bug report” as the topic and tell us which game you were playing, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and which device and browser you use.

Can I suggest a new game?

Yes. Choose “Game suggestion” and describe the idea. We cannot promise that every suggestion will be built, but we read them.

My high scores disappeared. What happened?

Scores and favorites are stored in your browser on your device. They can be removed if you clear your browser’s site data, use private browsing, or switch devices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I make a privacy request?

Choose “Privacy request” as the topic. Our Privacy Policy explains what we can help with.
