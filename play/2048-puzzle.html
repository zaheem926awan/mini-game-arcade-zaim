2048 Puzzle: Guide & Rules

2048 Puzzle is a sliding-tile number game on a 4 x 4 board. Every move slides all the tiles in one direction, and when two tiles with the same number collide they merge into one tile with double the value. Build a 2048 tile to win, or keep going for a higher score. This version includes a one-step undo.

How to play 2048 Puzzle

The game starts as soon as it opens, with two tiles on the board. Each new tile is a 2, or a 4 about one time in ten. After every move that changes the board, one new tile appears in a random empty cell. A move that changes nothing does not add a tile.

In a single move each tile can merge only once. If three equal tiles sit in a row, the two nearest the wall you are sliding towards merge, and the third stays put.

Dragging with the mouse on the board does not move tiles. Use the keyboard or click the D-pad buttons.

How a round ends

When a 2048 tile appears for the first time, an overlay says "YOU REACHED 2048!". You can choose KEEP PLAYING to carry on with the same board and keep scoring, or TRY AGAIN for a new game.

The game ends with "GAME OVER" when the board is full and no two neighbouring tiles match. The panel shows your score and best score, and Undo is still available from that screen.

Special features

  • One-step undo. Takes back your latest move, including its score.
  • Keep playing after 2048. The board is not cut off when you win.
  • The board itself is not saved when you leave, only your best score.

Tips for beginners

  • Undo right after a move you regret. It only goes back one move, and it cannot be used twice in a row.
  • Trying a direction that cannot move is harmless: no tile is added. But it counts as your latest step for Undo, so Undo after a blocked press will not take you back any further.
  • Slide towards the wall in a regular pattern. A common way to play is to keep your biggest tile in one corner, though the game does not require it.
  • Before a move, check rows and columns for pairs that will merge together, because each merge scores the new tile value.
  • Remember that a new tile appears after every move that works, so avoid filling the board with tiles that do not match.

2048 Puzzle FAQ

Do I have to stop at 2048?

No. When you reach 2048, choose KEEP PLAYING to carry on with the same board.

Can I undo more than one move?

No. Undo goes back one step only.

What new tiles appear?

A 2 most of the time (about 90%) and a 4 occasionally (about 10%).

What ends the game?

A full board with no two neighbouring tiles that match.

Is my progress saved if I leave?

No. Only your best score is saved, in this browser. The current board is not.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Swipe on the board or use the on-screen arrow buttons.
