Memory Match: Guide & Rules

Memory Match is a card-flipping game on a 4 x 4 grid with eight pairs of arcade symbols. Flip two cards at a time, remember where each symbol is hiding and clear the board in as few moves as you can. There is no move limit: your result is judged on the number of moves, with one to three stars.

How to play Memory Match

Each game shuffles 16 face-down cards. The eight symbols, each used on two cards, are 👾 🚀 💎 ⚡ 🎮 🍒 💣 and 👑.

Flipping two cards counts as one move. If the symbols match, both cards stay face up. If they do not match, they flip back after about three-quarters of a second, and you cannot flip anything else during that moment.

The timer starts counting seconds as soon as the board appears, not when you flip your first card, and stops when the last pair is matched. The time is shown in your results, but it does not affect your stars or your best score, and there is no time limit.

Scoring

EventResult
14 moves or fewer3 stars
15 to 20 moves2 stars
21 moves or more1 star

Your best result is the lowest number of moves, saved when you beat your previous best. The lowest possible is 8 moves, which would mean finding every pair on the first try.

How a round ends

The game is complete when all 8 pairs are matched. The "MEMORY GRID CLEARED!" screen shows your stars, moves, time and best number of moves. There is no way to lose.

Special features

  • Star rating. One to three stars based on the number of moves.
  • Shuffled every game. The layout is different each time.
  • Timer. Shown for your own interest, with no time pressure attached to it.

Tips for beginners

  • Early flips are for gathering information. Every card you see makes a later match easier.
  • When a first card reveals a symbol you have seen before, flip the card you remember to make a match.
  • Use the grid rows and columns to remember positions, for example "top row, second card".
  • Because stars depend on moves and not time, there is no reason to rush.
  • Pay attention to mismatches too. Both cards you just saw are information you can use on later turns.

Good to know

  • The timer cannot be paused. It starts when the board appears.

Memory Match FAQ

Is there a time limit?

No. The timer only records how long you took.

How are the stars decided?

By moves: 3 stars for 14 or fewer, 2 stars for 15 to 20, and 1 star for 21 or more.

What counts as one move?

Flipping two cards. It does not matter whether they match.

Does my best score track time or moves?

Moves. Your best is the fewest moves you have needed.

Is the layout the same every time?

No. The cards are shuffled at the start of every game and whenever you restart.

Can I use the keyboard?

No. Click or tap the cards.
