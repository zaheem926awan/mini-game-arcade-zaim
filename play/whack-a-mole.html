Whack-a-Mole: Guide & Rules

Whack-a-Mole is a 30-second reflex game on a 3 x 3 board. Moles, golden stars and bombs pop out of the holes for a moment. Hit the moles and stars as fast as you can, build a combo for bigger scores and keep your hands off the bombs.

How to play Whack-a-Mole

Every 0.45 to 0.9 seconds, something pops up in a random empty hole. Roughly 58% of the time it is a mole, 22% a golden star and 20% a bomb.

Moles stay up for about 0.9 to 1.3 seconds. Golden stars are quicker, at 0.75 seconds. Bombs stay for about 1.1 seconds.

Your combo counts successful hits in a row. From your second hit in a row, points are multiplied by 1.5, and from your fourth hit in a row they are multiplied by 2. Only hitting a bomb resets the combo. A mole or star that gets away does not break it, and clicking an empty hole does nothing.

Controls

InputWhat it does
MouseClick a mole, star or bomb to whack it.
TouchTap a mole, star or bomb.
P or EscPause, and press again to resume.
Buttons at the topPause and restart.

Apart from pausing, there are no keyboard controls in this game.

Scoring

EventResult
Mole+10 points (15 at combo 2 or 3, 20 at combo 4 and above)
Golden star+30 points (45 at combo 2 or 3, 60 at combo 4 and above)
Bomb-15 points (never below 0), and your combo resets to 0

Your best score is updated as it grows during the round.

How a round ends

The round ends when the 30-second timer reaches zero, with "TIME'S UP!". There is no losing screen. The panel shows your final score, max combo, accuracy and best score.

Accuracy here is the share of your whacks that were moles or stars rather than bombs. Escaped moles and clicks on empty holes are not counted.

Special features

  • Golden stars are worth three times a mole but stay up the shortest time.
  • Combo multiplier of 1.5 and then 2.
  • Bombs that cost points and break your streak.

Tips for beginners

  • Do not hit bombs. They are the only thing that resets your combo.
  • Missed moles are free, so skip a hole if hitting it would mean clicking a bomb.
  • Hit golden stars first when you see them. They disappear soonest and are worth the most.
  • Once you are at a combo of 4, every hit is worth double, so protect that streak.
  • Keep your pointer or finger near the middle of the board so that every hole is within quick reach.

Good to know

  • Pause with P or Esc and resume with the same key, or click the on-screen Resume button. The round carries on where you stopped and the 30-second clock stays frozen while paused. Moles that are showing when you pause may disappear in the meantime.

Whack-a-Mole FAQ

Do I lose points for missing a mole?

No. Only hitting a bomb costs points.

How does the combo multiplier work?

Each mole or star you hit in a row raises the combo. From your 2nd hit points are x1.5 and from your 4th they are x2. A bomb resets it.

What is accuracy?

The share of your whacks that were moles or stars rather than bombs. Missed moles do not count.

Can I use the keyboard?

No. Click or tap the holes.

How long is a round?

30 seconds.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device.
