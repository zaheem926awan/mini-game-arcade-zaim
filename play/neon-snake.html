Neon Snake: Guide & Rules

Neon Snake is a grid-based snake game. Steer a glowing snake around a 22 x 22 board, eat apples to grow, grab the golden star that appears after every fifth apple, and avoid the walls and your own tail. The snake speeds up as you eat, and a single crash ends the run.

How to play Neon Snake

You start with a snake of three cells heading right. There is one red apple on the board at a time. Eating it makes the snake one cell longer and scores points, and a new apple appears in a free cell.

After every fifth apple, a golden star appears in a random free cell and stays for 6 seconds. It is worth much more than an apple.

The snake moves one cell per step. A step takes 120 milliseconds at the start, and gets 4 milliseconds shorter for every 3 apples you eat, down to a fastest step of 65 milliseconds. The board has solid walls and does not wrap around. You cannot turn straight back on yourself, because a 180 degree turn is ignored.

Controls

The mouse cannot steer directly on the board. On a computer, use the keyboard, or click the D-pad buttons.

Scoring

EventResult
Apple+10 points, and the snake grows by one cell
Golden star+50 points, and the snake grows by one cell. A star does not count toward the next "every 5 apples" star.

Your best score is updated as soon as you beat it during a run.

How a round ends

The run ends when the head hits a wall ("Hit the electric boundary wall!") or the snake runs into its own body ("Ran into yourself!"). There is no winning screen: you play for a longer snake and a higher score.

The game-over panel shows your final score, snake length, apples eaten and your best score.

Special features

  • Golden star bonus. Appears after every 5th apple and lasts 6 seconds.
  • Speed scaling. The game gets faster as you eat, up to a fixed limit.
  • Live HUD. Score, best score and snake length are shown above the board.
  • There are no difficulty settings or extra modes in this game.

Tips for beginners

  • The first apples are the slowest part of a run. Use them to settle into a steady rhythm before the speed rises.
  • Count your apples. A star that is 50 points is worth five apples, but only pick it up if the route is safe within the 6 seconds.
  • Only one pending turn is stored per step, so tap a direction once and wait for the snake to move rather than pressing two keys at once.
  • As the snake grows, leave yourself an open lane to turn into. Long snakes end runs far more often than walls do.
  • On a phone, short decisive swipes work better than long drags.

Good to know

  • Pause with P or Esc and resume with the same key, or click the on-screen Resume button. Either way the run carries on exactly where you stopped.
  • A golden star's 6-second timer keeps running while the game is paused.

Neon Snake FAQ

Does the snake wrap around the edges?

No. Touching any wall ends the run.

How fast does the snake get?

It starts at one step every 120 milliseconds and speeds up by 4 milliseconds for every 3 apples, until it reaches 65 milliseconds per step.

When does the golden star appear?

After every 5th apple you eat, as long as a star is not already on the board. It disappears after 6 seconds.

Can I play with a mouse?

You can click the on-screen D-pad buttons. The mouse does not steer on the board itself.

What happens if I press the opposite direction?

Nothing. The snake cannot reverse straight into itself, so that input is ignored.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device.
