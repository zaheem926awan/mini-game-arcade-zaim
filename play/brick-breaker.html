Brick Breaker: Guide & Rules

Brick Breaker is a breakout game. Slide a paddle along the bottom of the board, bounce a ball into rows of coloured bricks and clear three stages of increasing size. Falling capsules can add balls, widen your paddle, give bonus points or slow the ball. You have three lives, and the ball launches the moment you start.

How to play Brick Breaker

Press LAUNCH BALL and the ball starts moving straight away, and after you lose a life the next ball also launches immediately. Get your paddle into position first.

There are 8 columns of bricks. Stage 1 has five rows. Stage 2 has six rows with a checkerboard pattern of gaps from the third row down. Stage 3 has seven rows with a gap in the middle of the third and fourth rows. When a stage is cleared, the next layout is built and the ball and paddle reset. Your score and remaining lives carry over.

Brick colour tells you how tough it is: gold bricks take 3 hits, cyan 2, red 1, green 1 and purple 2. The colours repeat in that order down the rows, and bricks that take more than one hit show the hits remaining as a small number.

Where the ball lands on the paddle sets its bounce angle: near the centre sends it almost straight up, near either edge sends it off at a steep angle (up to 70 degrees). Each paddle bounce also makes the ball slightly faster (about 2%) up to a speed limit.

Controls

InputWhat it does
MouseMove the pointer over the board. The paddle follows it. No clicking is needed.
Left / Right arrows or A / DMove the paddle.
P or EscPause, and press again to resume.
Space or EnterPlay again after a game over or after clearing the final stage.
TouchDrag a finger across the board, or hold the MOVE LEFT and MOVE RIGHT buttons under it.
Buttons at the topPause and restart.

The MOVE LEFT and MOVE RIGHT buttons respond to touch. On a computer, use the mouse or the keyboard.

Scoring

EventResult
Each hit on a brick+15 points
Destroying a brick+25 more points. A 1-hit brick is worth 40 in total, a 2-hit brick 55 and a 3-hit brick 70.
Star capsule+75 points

Destroyed bricks drop a capsule 22% of the time. Four kinds exist and each is equally likely. Catch one with the paddle to use it; uncaught capsules fall away.

How a round ends

You lose a life only when every ball in play has fallen off the bottom. With three lives gone, the run ends with "GAME OVER". Lives are not refilled between stages.

Clearing all bricks on stage 3 ends the game with "STAGE CLEAR!", the game's winning screen. The panel shows your score, the stage reached and your best score, and your best is saved when a run ends.

Special features

  • Multi-ball (lightning bolt capsule). Adds two extra balls.
  • Wide paddle (arrow capsule). Widens the paddle from 90 to 140 pixels for 10 seconds.
  • Bonus (star capsule). +75 points.
  • Slow (turtle capsule). Cuts the ball's current speed by 25% when caught. It builds back up gradually as the ball keeps bouncing off the paddle.
  • None of the capsules is harmful.

Tips for beginners

  • Aim with the paddle edges. Hitting the ball with the edge sends it sideways, which is how you reach bricks in the corners.
  • With multi-ball you only lose a life when the last ball falls, so it is the safest capsule to chase.
  • Check the numbers on bricks. A 3-hit gold brick takes three hits, so do not leave it behind while chasing easier ones.
  • Long rallies get faster, because each paddle bounce speeds the ball up a little.
  • Use the wide paddle window of 10 seconds to catch awkward angles and collect more capsules.

Good to know

  • Pause with P or Esc and resume with the same key, or click the on-screen Resume button. Either way the run carries on exactly where you stopped.

Brick Breaker FAQ

How many stages are there?

Three. Clearing every brick on the third stage wins the game.

How many lives do I get?

Three. A life is lost when all balls in play have fallen.

Do any capsules hurt me?

No. The four capsules are multi-ball, wide paddle, bonus points and slow ball.

Can I use the keyboard and the mouse?

Yes, both move the paddle. Touch screens use finger drag or the on-screen buttons.

Do my score and lives carry over between stages?

Yes. Only the ball and paddle reset when a new stage begins.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device, when a run ends.
