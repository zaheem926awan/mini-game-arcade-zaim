Endless Runner: Guide & Rules

Endless Runner is a side-scrolling dash across neon rooftops. Your runner moves forward on its own, and you decide when to jump, double jump or slide. Leap over spikes, duck under laser drones, collect energy crystals and see how far you can get before a single collision ends the run.

How to play Endless Runner

The runner starts at a steady pace and speeds up as your distance grows, until it reaches a top speed after about 4,400 m. The gap between obstacles gets shorter at higher speeds.

Two obstacles appear, at random. Orange spikes sit on the ground and must be jumped over. Red laser drones hover at head height and must be slid under. Roughly 55% of obstacles are spikes and 45% are drones.

About 70% of the time, a trail of three energy crystals follows an obstacle. After a drone the crystals sit close to the ground, so you collect them as you run or slide. After a spike they hang at jump height, so you collect them with a jump.

You can jump twice before landing (a double jump), and the counter refills when you touch the ground. Jumping while sliding cancels the slide. The collision box is slightly forgiving, so very close misses do not always count as hits.

Scoring

Score = distance + 25 for every crystal. The top bar shows distance, crystals, score and best. Your best is saved during the run, as soon as you beat it.

How a round ends

Touching a spike or drone ends the run with "RUN TERMINATED". There is no finish line, because the goal is to go as far as you can.

The panel shows distance, crystals, score and your best score.

Special features

  • Double jump with two jumps before landing.
  • Slide to pass under drones, and hold it for as long as you need.
  • Speed scaling that makes later sections faster.
  • The game has one level with no modes or settings.

Tips for beginners

  • Start your slide just before a drone reaches you and hold it until it has passed.
  • Use the double jump on a tight spike so that you do not land on the next obstacle.
  • Watch where the crystals are. A trail high in the air after a spike is picked up by jumping.
  • Three crystals are worth 75 points, so they are worth a small detour but not a collision.
  • On a phone, tap the game area to jump. It is quicker than reaching for the JUMP button.

Good to know

  • Pause with P or Esc and resume with the same key, or click the on-screen Resume button. Either way the run carries on exactly where you stopped.

Endless Runner FAQ

Can I play Endless Runner with a mouse?

No. The game area and the on-screen buttons use touch events only, so on a computer you need the keyboard.

How does the double jump work?

You get two jumps in a row. Press jump again while in the air. The counter refills as soon as you land.

What do I do about the red drones?

Slide under them by holding the down arrow or S, or by holding the SLIDE button on a touch screen.

How is the score calculated?

Distance plus 25 points for each crystal you collect.

Does the game get harder?

Yes. The runner speeds up with distance until it reaches a maximum speed, and obstacles appear closer together.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device.
