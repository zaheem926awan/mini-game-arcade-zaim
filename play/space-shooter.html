How to play Space Shooter

Every run is a mission. Press Launch to continue the campaign, or pick any unlocked mission on the Mission Board. Your ship starts at the bottom of the screen. Each mission has one objective, such as destroying a number of enemies, surviving for a set time or defeating the sector boss, plus an optional bonus objective that pays extra coins.

Your shield is your health. An enemy bolt, a lightning strike, a beam or a collision costs 1 shield point and gives you 1.25 seconds of invulnerability. When the shield reaches zero the ship is destroyed and the mission fails, but the coins you picked up are kept.

Later sectors are harder. Enemy health starts at 1.00x in sector 1 and rises to 1.80x in sector 6, each later mission in a sector adds 6%, and enemies shoot faster. Spawns also speed up as a mission goes on.

Between runs, use the menu tabs: Hangar to buy and equip ships, Upgrades to spend coins on permanent boosts, Missions to choose a sector, and Medals to track milestones.

Elite enemies

After the first 45 seconds of a run, any enemy except an asteroid can spawn as an elite, with a gold ring around it. Elites are 20% larger, have 2.4 times the health, are worth 3 times the points and 4 times the coins, shoot more often, and always drop a power-up. The chance grows from 3.5% of spawns in sector 1 to about 24% in sector 6.

Bosses

Each sector ends with a boss mission. About half a minute in, a warning appears and the boss arrives with a health bar across the top of the screen. At 66% and 33% health it enters a new phase: its bolts are cleared, it pauses briefly and its patterns get tougher.

How a round ends

A mission is complete the moment its objective is met (for boss missions, when the boss is destroyed). A short victory pause clears the screen of enemy fire. A mission fails when your shield reaches zero.

The results screen shows your score, kills, elites, time, accuracy, best combo, hits taken and an itemised coin breakdown, plus any medals you unlocked. Free Flight has no objective: it ends only when your ship is destroyed.

Power-ups and medals

  • Power-up drops. About 6% of destroyed enemies (2% of asteroids) drop one, every elite drops one and each boss drops two. Fly into it to collect it.
  • Rapid Fire. 65% more fire rate for 9 seconds.
  • Spread Shot. Four extra angled shots on every volley for 10 seconds.
  • Overshield. A bubble for 8 seconds that absorbs hits. Each absorbed hit uses up 1.5 seconds of it.
  • Repair. Restores 2 shield points, up to your maximum.
  • Ability Charge. Refills your special ability instantly.

Tips for beginners

  • Shoot snipers and gunships first. They are the enemies that fire back, and a sniper shows a warning line before it shoots.
  • Avoid ramming enemies. Collisions cost a shield point, and a kamikaze that reaches you is destroyed but still hurts.
  • Letting an enemy escape off the bottom is free, so never risk your shield for one you cannot reach.
  • Spend your first coins on Shield Strength. Extra points forgive mistakes while you learn each boss pattern.
  • Save your special ability for boss phase changes or crowded screens, and grab Ability Charge pickups to use it again.
  • Watch for the warning columns in sectors 2 and 5. A strike lands only after the column has pulsed for about one second.
  • In boss fights, move toward the gaps. Bullet walls always leave an opening.

Good to know

  • Pause with P or Esc and resume with the same key, or click the on-screen Resume button. Either way the run carries on exactly where you stopped, including every cooldown and power-up timer.
  • Your progress is saved in your browser using local storage on your device. Coins you collect are saved as you play, so refreshing the page does not lose them.
  • If you played before the campaign update, your best score is kept and you start with a coin bonus based on it, up to 400 coins.
  • The game pauses by itself when you switch to another tab, and it respects your system's reduced-motion setting by toning down screen shake and particles.

Space Shooter FAQ

How do I shoot with a mouse?

Move the pointer over the board and the ship follows it. Hold the left mouse button to fire, or hold Space or Enter. On a phone, drag to fly and the ship fires automatically.

How much health do I have?

Your Vanguard starts with a shield of 3 points. The Striker has 2, the Bulwark 5 and the Nova and Phantom 3, and Shield Strength upgrades add up to 3 more. Each enemy bolt, lightning strike, beam or collision removes one point, and a repair power-up restores 2.

Do enemies that get past me hurt?

No. Only enemy bolts, hazards, boss attacks and collisions reduce your shield.

How do I unlock new ships?

Earn coins and buy ships in the Hangar: Striker 500, Bulwark 1,500, Nova 3,500 and Phantom 7,000 coins. A ship you have not bought cannot be equipped. The Vanguard is free.

How do I earn coins?

Destroy enemies and pick up the gold coins they drop, clear missions for a coin reward, complete each mission's bonus objective, and score points (1 coin per 400 points at the end of a run). Coins you pick up are kept even if the mission fails.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your coins, unlocked ships, selected ship, upgrades, mission progress, medals and best score are saved in your browser on your own device. They are not sent anywhere. If you clear your browser data, your progress is cleared too.

What happens when I pause?

Pressing P or Esc, or the pause button, freezes everything. Resume continues the same run with your score, shield, power-ups and cooldowns exactly as they were. Holding the key down does not flicker between paused and running.

Can I replay missions?

Yes. Replays pay 30% of the mission reward (at least 10 coins), and the first time you complete a bonus objective it pays in full. Free Flight is an endless mode for chasing a high score in any sector you have unlocked.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Drag your finger to fly: the ship moves with your finger without being hidden under it, and fires automatically while you drag. A large Ability button and a Fire button sit under the board.
