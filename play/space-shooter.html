Space Shooter: Guide & Rules

Space Shooter is a vertical shooter. Fly a starfighter around the screen, shoot drones, heavy cruisers and tumbling asteroids, and stay alive for as long as your three shield points last. Waves get busier as your score climbs, and rare power-ups give you a triple shot, a shield point or a screen-clearing bomb.

How to play Space Shooter

Your ship starts at the bottom centre and fires two parallel shots from twin cannons, one volley every 180 milliseconds while the fire control is held. You can fly anywhere across the board, within limits.

Three kinds of enemy arrive from the top, in roughly these proportions: drones 45%, cruisers 30% and asteroids 25%.

The wave number is your score divided by 350 (rounded down) plus one. A higher wave makes enemies arrive more often: the gap between arrivals starts at about 1.4 seconds and shortens by 0.12 seconds per wave, down to a minimum of 0.7 seconds. The enemies themselves do not get faster.

A cruiser bolt or a collision with any enemy costs 1 shield point and gives you 1.2 seconds of invulnerability. An enemy you collide with is destroyed, but you get no points for it. Enemies that fly past the bottom of the screen cost you nothing.

How a round ends

The run ends with "SHIP DESTROYED" when your shield reaches zero. There is no winning screen: it is an endless score chase.

The panel shows your score, the wave you reached and your best score.

Special features

  • Power-up drops. About 16% of destroyed enemies drop a power-up, and the three kinds are equally likely. Fly into one to collect it.
  • Triple shot. A three-way spread of lasers for 9 seconds.
  • Shield. Restores 1 shield point, up to the maximum of 3.
  • Bomb. Clears all enemies on screen the moment you pick it up.

Tips for beginners

  • Shoot cruisers first. They are the only enemies that fire back, and they take three hits.
  • Avoid ramming enemies. Collisions cost a shield point and give you no score.
  • Letting an enemy escape off the bottom is free, so never risk your shield for a drone you cannot reach.
  • Use the 1.2 seconds of invulnerability after a hit to reposition, not to take on more risk.
  • A bomb power-up fires as soon as you touch it. If you can, collect it when the screen is crowded to get the most points.
  • Triple shot covers a wider area, which helps against asteroids and drones that drift sideways.

Good to know

  • Pause with P or Esc and resume with the same key. The on-screen Resume button currently restarts the run, so on a phone it is best not to pause mid-run.

Space Shooter FAQ

How do I shoot with a mouse?

Moving the mouse flies the ship, but it does not fire. Hold Space or Enter, or hold the on-screen FIRE button.

How much health do I have?

A shield of 3 points. Each enemy bolt or collision removes one. A shield power-up restores one point, up to 3.

Do enemies that get past me hurt?

No. Only enemy bolts and collisions reduce your shield.

What does the bomb power-up do?

It destroys every enemy currently on screen and adds their points to your score.

What do the waves change?

Waves make enemies arrive more frequently. Enemy speed and strength stay the same.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Drag your finger to fly and the ship fires automatically while you drag.
