Fruit Slice: Guide & Rules

Fruit Slice is a 60-second blade game. Fruit flies up from the bottom of the screen and you slice it by dragging a mouse or swiping a finger straight through it. Slice several fruits in one stroke for combo bonuses, stay away from the bombs, and see how high you can score before the clock runs out.

How to play Fruit Slice

When you open the game, press its own PLAY NOW button. A short 3-2-1 countdown runs, then fruit is launched in waves that arc up and fall back under gravity. The fruit you will see is watermelon, orange, strawberry, apple, banana, pineapple and kiwi, plus the occasional golden apple.

The level rises every 12 seconds of play, up to Level 6. Higher levels send more fruit per wave, make fruit fall slightly faster and raise the chance that a bomb is mixed in. From Level 3, a wave can turn into a "FRUIT FRENZY": five fruits launched in quick succession across the screen.

Bombs only start appearing after the first 4 seconds of a round, and only while the Hazard Bombs setting is on.

A swipe combo is paid out when your finger or mouse button is released, or about 0.4 seconds after your last slice.

How a round ends

A round ends when the timer reaches zero ("TIME'S UP"), or when you have lost all 3 lives to bombs ("NO LIVES LEFT"). Fruit that you miss costs nothing: no points, no time and no lives.

There is no winning screen, because the goal is a score. At the end you see your final score, fruits sliced, best combo and bombs hit, together with a rank title based on your score:

  • Fruit Rookie: 0 and up
  • Juice Apprentice: 15 and up
  • Blade Adept: 35 and up
  • Slice Master: 60 and up
  • Fruit Ninja: 90 and up
  • Legendary Slicer: 130 and up

Special features

  • Hazard Bombs on or off. Turn bombs off from the start screen or the bomb button to practise without them. The game calls this Zen Mode. The setting is remembered in your browser, and scores in this mode count toward the same best score.
  • Four blade colours. Your choice is remembered in your browser.
  • Slow motion. Big combos (4 or more fruits) and bomb hits briefly slow the action down.
  • Music and sound effects with a mute button.

Tips for beginners

  • Combos count fruits per swipe, so one long stroke through a cluster is worth more than several short ones.
  • Do not lunge for every fruit. A missed fruit is free, while a bomb costs points, time and a life.
  • Look before you cut. Bombs start after 4 seconds and become more likely as the level climbs.
  • Golden apples are rare and worth +5 and 2 extra seconds, so they are worth a deliberate swipe.
  • Strawberries, pineapples and kiwis are worth 2 points, double the other fruit.
  • Keep the pointer moving. Slicing needs real movement, not just touching a fruit.

Good to know

  • A bomb also costs one of your 3 lives. The in-game How to Play card only mentions the points and time penalty.

Fruit Slice FAQ

Is Fruit Slice free to play?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no download and no sign-up.

How many lives do I have?

Three. You only lose a life by slicing a bomb. Missing fruit does not cost a life.

What does the golden apple do?

Slicing it gives +5 points and adds 2 seconds to the timer. It appears rarely.

Can I play without bombs?

Yes. Switch off Hazard Bombs on the start screen, or press the bomb button while playing. Scores earned this way still count toward your best score.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Swipe your finger across the fruit. The page will not scroll while you are touching the game area.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device. It is not shared online and it is cleared if you clear your browser data.
