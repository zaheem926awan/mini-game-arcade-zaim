Block Puzzle: Guide & Rules

Block Puzzle is a falling-block game on a 10 x 20 board. Seven shapes drop one at a time. Move and rotate each one so that it completes a full row, which then clears. A ghost outline shows where the piece will land, one piece can be put on hold, and the pace quickens every ten lines.

How to play Block Puzzle

Each new piece is chosen at random from the seven shapes, so the same shape can come up several times in a row. The NEXT box on the right shows the one piece that will follow, and the HOLD box on the left shows your held piece. An outline shows where the current piece would land if you dropped it.

Pieces fall by themselves. At level 1 a piece drops one row every 800 milliseconds. Each level makes this 70 milliseconds faster, down to 120 milliseconds from level 11. You move up a level every 10 lines.

Rotation turns the piece clockwise. If a piece would overlap something after rotating, the game tries shifting it one cell left or right to make it fit.

You can hold a piece once per turn. The first hold stores the current piece and brings in the next one. After that, a hold swaps the current piece with the held piece. You cannot hold again until the next piece has been locked in place.

Controls

InputWhat it does
Left / Right arrows or A / DMove the piece one cell sideways.
Up arrow or WRotate clockwise.
Down arrow or SSoft drop: move down one row. If the piece is already resting, it locks in place.
SpaceHard drop: drop the piece to its landing spot and lock it.
C or ShiftHold or swap the held piece.
P or EscPause, and press again to resume.
Space or EnterPlay again after a game over.
On-screen buttonsLeft, rotate, right, hold, soft drop and hard drop. They respond to touch and to clicks.

There are no swipe gestures on the board. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

Scoring

EventResult
Soft drop+1 point per row
Hard drop+2 points per row dropped
1 line cleared100 x your level
2 lines cleared at once300 x your level
3 lines cleared at once500 x your level
4 lines cleared at once800 x your level

Your best score is updated when you clear lines. Because the line values are multiplied by your level, line clears are worth far more than drop points.

How a round ends

The game ends with "BLOCK MATRIX FULL" when a new piece has no room to appear at the top of the board. There is no winning screen: it is an endless score chase.

The panel shows your score, lines cleared, level and best score.

Special features

  • Ghost outline of the landing spot.
  • One hold slot for saving an awkward piece.
  • Next piece preview. One piece is shown, not a queue.
  • Levels. The speed rises every 10 lines.

Tips for beginners

  • Use the ghost outline to check the landing spot before you drop.
  • Four lines at once is worth 800 x level, compared with 400 x level for four separate single lines. Leaving one column open and waiting for a long piece is the usual way to set it up.
  • Hold a piece you cannot place well and use it later, but remember you can hold only once per turn.
  • Press Space once per piece. Holding the key down may repeat and drop the next pieces straight away.
  • Keep the stack low and flat. The game is over as soon as a new piece cannot fit at the top.

Good to know

  • Pause with P or Esc and resume with the same key, or click the on-screen Resume button. Either way the run carries on exactly where you stopped.

Block Puzzle FAQ

How many next pieces can I see?

One. The NEXT box shows only the piece that follows the current one.

Are the pieces random?

Yes. Each piece is chosen at random, so the same shape can appear more than once in a row.

Can I rotate the other way?

No. Rotation is clockwise only.

How do levels work?

You gain a level every 10 lines. Each level makes pieces fall 70 milliseconds faster, down to a limit of 120 milliseconds.

How many pieces can I hold?

One. You can hold once per turn, and you can swap with the held piece after the next piece locks.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, using the on-screen buttons below the board.
