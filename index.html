Neo Arcade Vault

Play 10 free browser mini-games: Fruit Slice, Neon Snake, Brick Breaker, Space Shooter, 2048 Puzzle, Tic-Tac-Toe, Memory Match, Endless Runner, Block Puzzle and Whack-a-Mole. No downloads and no sign-up.

The arcade needs JavaScript. Please enable it in your browser, or read more on our Games, About and Contact pages.

What is Neo Arcade Vault?

Neo Arcade Vault is a free, independent collection of ten mini-games that run directly in your web browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create: open the page, pick a game and start playing on a phone, tablet or computer.

Depending on the game, you play with a mouse, a touch screen, the keyboard or on-screen buttons. Your best scores and favorite games are saved in your own browser on your own device, not on a server.

Every game also has its own guide page with a scoring table, the full list of controls, beginner tips and answers to common questions, including details that are easy to miss while playing.

The ten games and their guides

The vault covers five categories: action, arcade, puzzle, strategy and casual. Some rounds are over in half a minute, while others last until your first mistake. Open a guide to learn the rules before you play.

  • Fruit Slice Action

    A 60-second blade game. Swipe through flying fruit, link several slices in one stroke for combo bonuses, and steer clear of the bombs, which cost you points, time and a life.

    Fruit Slice guide: rules, controls and tips

  • Neon Snake Arcade

    Steer a glowing snake around a 22 x 22 grid, eat apples to grow and grab the golden star that appears after every fifth apple. The snake speeds up as it eats, and one crash ends the run.

    Neon Snake guide: rules, controls and tips

  • Brick Breaker Arcade

    Slide a paddle, bounce the ball into rows of bricks and clear three stages. Falling capsules can add balls, widen the paddle, give bonus points or slow the ball down.

    Brick Breaker guide: rules, controls and tips

  • Space Shooter Action

    Fly a starfighter against drones, heavy cruisers and tumbling asteroids with three shield points to spare. Rare power-ups add a triple shot, a shield point or a screen-clearing bomb.

    Space Shooter guide: rules, controls and tips

  • 2048 Puzzle Puzzle

    Slide tiles around a 4 x 4 board and merge matching numbers to build a 2048 tile, or keep going for a higher score. A one-step undo helps you recover from a bad move.

    2048 Puzzle guide: rules, controls and tips

  • Tic-Tac-Toe Strategy

    Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Invincible, or take turns with a friend on the same device. A quick round of the classic three-in-a-row duel.

    Tic-Tac-Toe guide: rules, controls and tips

  • Memory Match Casual

    Find all eight pairs of symbols on a 4 x 4 grid. There is no time limit: you earn one to three stars depending on how few moves you need.

    Memory Match guide: rules, controls and tips

  • Endless Runner Action

    Your runner dashes across neon rooftops by itself while you time jumps, double jumps and slides over spikes and under laser drones. Collect energy crystals; one collision ends the run.

    Endless Runner guide: rules, controls and tips

  • Block Puzzle Puzzle

    Rotate and drop seven kinds of falling blocks on a 10 x 20 board to clear full rows. A ghost outline, a hold slot and a hard drop help you plan, and the pace quickens every ten lines.

    Block Puzzle guide: rules, controls and tips

  • Whack-a-Mole Casual

    A 30-second reflex game on a 3 x 3 board. Hit moles and golden stars, chain hits for a combo multiplier and keep your hands off the bombs.

    Whack-a-Mole guide: rules, controls and tips
